# Denis Zaharia - Design Brief

Design read: A cinematic personal brand for a hands-on entrepreneur, with calm authority, real operating history and a forward-looking AI chapter.
Concept spine: STILL BUILDING. One continuous dark architectural world turns years of building into a forward camera journey.
Delivery tier: spectacle. Cinematic scroll scrub, kinetic typography, restrained cursor physics and a second editorial beat.
Animation mode: animated-website
Journey shape: single-shot. One continuous orbit is the strongest identity-first hero and avoids artificial seams.
Journey: Hero / Denis Zaharia / I don't follow the next wave. I build on it. / Entrepreneur, Business Builder, AI Founder.
Journey: Systems / 2009 / The first system. / Forex is framed as an early lesson in discipline and decision-making.
Journey: Internet / 2010 / The internet changed the rules. / Online business becomes the next learning system.
Journey: Business / 2011 onward / I kept building. / Education turns into execution across real businesses.
Journey: Convergence / 2026 / AI changes the speed. / KEVOT is the next chapter, not a sudden reinvention.
World grammar: Ink-black stone, smoked glass, brushed dark metal, subtle warm wood, cream type, controlled cobalt-blue rim light, locked exposure, premium architectural office.
Mobile framing: Subject stays center-safe, typography moves to lower and upper safe zones, 720p mobile encode, shorter copy.
Delivery budget: Desktop film <=32 MiB. Mobile film <=16 MiB.
Journey enactment: The camera physically circles Denis while the story advances from systems to business to AI, making the person the constant while the chapters change.
Locked palette: #080A0D ink, #F1EEE7 bone, #3156D8 cobalt blue, #8A909B muted. Blue is explicitly user-requested and kept deep, non-neon and non-cyan.
Locked type: Cabinet Grotesk style display with Inter Tight style body and IBM Plex Mono style metadata, implemented with available system-safe fallbacks if licensed webfonts are unavailable.
Section plan: full-viewport scrub hero; vertical timeline; oversized stats; asymmetric project ledger; KEVOT manifesto; warm editorial Beyond Business; fullscreen contact finale.
Asset plan: one identity-driven hero film with exact posters; identity portrait stills; architectural section plates; DZ monogram; custom line icon set; OG cover and favicon.
CTA inventory: BUILD WITH ME is a cobalt framed square-corner action with expanding blue field. DISCOVER KEVOT is an underlined text gateway with directional motion. Project links use edge-reveal arrows.
Brand: DENIS ZAHARIA. ENTREPRENEUR / BUSINESS BUILDER / AI FOUNDER.
Geography: MOLDOVA -> ITALY -> WORLDWIDE.
Primary domain: deniszaharia.com.
Secondary entity: KEVOT, kevot.ai.
Languages: EN, IT, RO, RU. English is canonical master.
Tone: short, direct, human, credible. No guru language, no fake proof, no invented financial claims.
Approved proof: 10+ years in business; 10+ businesses built; 10,000+ people served; 5 countries.
Projects: Aeropark, Travel Moldova, E-commerce ventures, KEVOT.
Personal beat: Beyond Business is brief and tasteful. Family is a value, not a marketing asset.
